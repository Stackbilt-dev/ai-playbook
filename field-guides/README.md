# Field Guides

Field guides turn related AI Playbook patterns into one opinionated, end-to-end practice. Frameworks explain reusable structures; field guides show how to apply several structures to a recurring class of real decisions.

## Editorial Contract

Every guide should:

1. teach portable principles rather than one vendor stack;
2. name the failure mode before prescribing a pattern;
3. distinguish observations, inferences, preferences, and obligations;
4. explain tradeoffs and counterexamples;
5. include inspectable worksheets or review artifacts;
6. state what would falsify its recommendations;
7. keep experimental behavior non-authoritative until evidence supports promotion.

Field guides are not standards or guarantees. Adapt their review questions to the consequences, affected people, and evidence available in your domain.
